Citation: Roark, Brian; Wolf-Sonkin, Lawrence; Kirov, Christo; Mielke,
Sabrina J.; Johny, Cibu; Demirsahin, Isin; Hall, Keith (2020).
Processing South Asian Languages Written in the Latin Script: the Dakshina
Dataset. LREC 2020, pp. 2413-2423. https://aclanthology.org/2020.lrec-1.294/

Modifications by TypeRight: lowercase/filter Latin forms; merge attested
romanizations into word-family metadata; add existing authored conversational
entries; join native-script usage proxies; train smoothed character n-gram
language evidence. These derived data assets are distributed under CC BY-SA 4.0.
Annotation vote counts are recorded separately from word-frequency proxies.
Source member and output SHA256 values appear in provenance.json.

Conversational examples in tools/data/manglish-conversations.tsv are fictional,
authored fixtures, not messages collected from users. They supply conversational
priors and an independently partitioned template holdout, not a claim of real
world typing accuracy or Gboard equivalence.
